# ADR 0003: BPSec Ed25519 BIB & X25519+AES-256-GCM BCB with Explicit Policy Modes

- **Status**: Accepted
- **Scope**: `dtnpm-core` (`src/security.rs`, `src/config/mod.rs`)

## Context
In amateur radio (where unencrypted third-party traffic regulations may apply depending on jurisdiction and mode), operators frequently need cryptographic integrity/authentication (BIB) without mandatory payload confidentiality (BCB), as well as staged migration where legacy unsigned nodes still interoperate during testing or closed RF nets.

## Decision
Implement RFC 9172 / RFC 9173 directional keystores supporting Ed25519 BIB signatures and X25519+AES-256-GCM BCB encryption (`DirectionalKeyStore`). Expose `VerifyPolicy` (`Strict`, `Warn`, `None`):
- `Strict`: enforces cryptographic authentication (`AuthStatus::Valid`) and drops unverified bundles in participating services.
- `Warn` / `None`: explicit opt-in modes for development or pre-secured links that log warnings or skip verification cleanly.

## Consequences
- Preserves out-of-the-box RF interoperability where legally required while enabling strict end-to-end authentication when configured.
